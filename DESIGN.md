# Industrial Utility System

A high-visibility, high-contrast design system optimized for industrial warehouse environments and rugged Android scanning devices.

## Colors
- **Surface**: `#f8f9ff` (Primary background)
- **Surface-Dim**: `#cbdbf5`
- **Surface-Container**: `#eff4ff` (Cards and navigation containers)
- **Primary**: `#0f172a` (Deep slate for primary actions and headings)
- **Secondary**: `#506071`
- **Error**: `#ba1a1a` (Critical stock alerts)
- **Outline**: `#74777f` (Border and divider definition)

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Work Sans
- **Headlines**: Semi-bold, optimized for readability in variable lighting.
- **Body**: Regular, focused on data density and clear SKU/LOC labeling.

## Components & Patterns
- **Roundness**: `ROUND_FOUR` (4px radius) for a professional, structured feel.
- **Touch Targets**: Large, accessible buttons (min 48dp) for gloved or fast-paced operation.
- **Hierarchy**: Heavy use of borders and elevation to separate distinct scanning tasks and data sets.
